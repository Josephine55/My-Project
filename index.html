ABOUT US

We are the IADC Developer's Team, a group of students turning classroom coding into real solutions for our school. We built this Records Hub to make school record keeping simple, secure and accessible for everyone. Through this work, we are not only developing our coding skills, but also learning teamwork, problem solving, and how to create tools that people actually use.

MISSION

To apply technology to real world problems, and learn coding by building practical, usable projects

VISION

To create a culture where students don't just study technology, but build with it,turning ideas into solutions that makes a difference
